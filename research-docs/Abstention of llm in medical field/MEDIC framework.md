## *Summary :*
### This paper proposes an evaluation framework that takes into consideration the question - *Is performance of LLMs on standardized medical tests really the only determiner of operational and usage reliability for clinical meeds?* 
### The new framework consists of tasks based on : *M*edical Reasoning, *E*thical and Bias Concerns, *D*ata and Language Understanding, *I*n-context Learning, *C*linical Safety, **MEDIC**
### MCQs only test whether model can choose a correct answer from a set of answers. But operational tasks in healthcare require broader range of decision making ability. 
### *Knowledge != Execution*

###  They categorized safety:
- ### Active safety (Detecting an error or mistake and identifying) and
- ### Passive safety (Not answering something dangerous)
### They have a Cross Examination step to validate the response from multiple angles.

### *Their finding :* They find substantial **cross-dimensional trade-offs**. Meaning, performance varies task wise. Concluding to *model selection should depend on the **specific clinical application***

## *How this connects to our work :*
### This tells us, **different safety behaviors are separable**. **High performance on one capability or safety behavior does not imply competence in another.**
